I

Reading without email

You do not need to provide an email address or create an account to read public dossiers or search the portal.

A free request is a separate action. The form asks for your question and email address for a reply. The language, date, request ID and status are also stored.

II

Where a request goes

The question and email address are stored in the portal's working database and forwarded to the project team through a Telegram bot. If a dossier is prepared, a link is sent to that email address through a mail service. Those services receive the information needed to deliver the messages.

We use the address to reply to the request and do not send marketing emails. Submitting the form does not guarantee that a dossier will be prepared or that a reply will arrive by a particular date.

III

What not to send

Do not include names, document numbers, addresses, health information or other details unnecessary to frame the legal question. A description without identifying people is enough.

Do not send documents or information that needs confidential advice through the form. A free request does not create a lawyer-client relationship.

IV

Retention

The working database has a procedure to remove the email address and question from a free request after 180 days. Regular execution must be configured when the portal is published; until then, this period cannot be guaranteed.

Cleaning the portal database does not delete messages already delivered through Telegram or the mail service. Retention there depends on those services' settings and the project team.
