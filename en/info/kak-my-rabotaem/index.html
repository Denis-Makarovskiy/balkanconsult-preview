I

What we do

We gather sources of Montenegrin law around specific questions and explain what we could and could not establish. Each dossier lists the sources used and the date they were checked.

Public dossiers are created entirely by AI and have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Other language versions are machine generated and have not been edited.

II

Where the rules come from

We use official Službeni list texts, government pages and the register of acts. A record helps locate a source, but does not by itself establish the full current text with every amendment.

III

How a dossier is built

A dossier answers one question and always consists of five parts in the same order. That way you can see where the rule is and where the gap is.

  1. I
    In short — what the rules say about the scenario.
  2. II
    Rules — in the original, with the edition and a link.
  3. III
    Conditions and deadlines — what the application of the rule depends on.
  4. IV
    Not established — what is missing from the sources.
  5. V
    Questions for a lawyer — what the text of the rule does not settle.
IV

Where the boundary is

The law of Montenegro classifies the giving of legal counsel and opinions as legal assistance and reserves it for lawyers. That is why the portal does not assess a person's situation. It shows the rules and formulates questions you can take to a lawyer.

«давање правних савјета и мишљења»
Zakon o advokaturi, čl. 3 st. 1 t. 1 · original
the giving of legal counsel and opinionstranslation
  • we do not assess a person's situation and do not give legal opinions;
  • we do not file documents and do not represent anyone before authorities;
  • we do not promise the outcome of an authority's decision;
  • we do not produce certified translations: every translation is marked as a translation.
V

What is not covered yet

The source collection is still growing. Some amendments, municipal rules, transition provisions or current forms have not yet been collected. Each dossier identifies these gaps under “Not established”.

  • an act record and its full current text require different levels of verification;
  • local rules and procedures depend on the municipality and date;
  • changes after a dossier's stated check date are not reflected.
