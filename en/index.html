What a dossier is

A public dossier addresses a typical question in five parts and shows the legal basis and its limits.

  1. IWhat is the answer?A direct answer to the main question and the limits of that answer.
  2. IIWhat supports the answer?Act, provision, version and a link to the source text.
  3. IIIWhat does it depend on?What the answer depends on, and what is required and when.
  4. IVWhat remains unknown?What the sources do not establish. Gaps are shown.
  5. VWhat should you check before acting?Documents, dates and exceptions to check before acting.
