I

In short

Supported with limits

For 2026, Montenegro’s corporate income tax uses progressive 9%, 12% and 15% bands of taxable profit under Article 28; the formula is not a flat rate on turnover [1]. The amendment published as 104/26 applies from 1 January 2027, so it is not used as the 2026 calculation rule [2,3].[1], [2]

The bands are a 2026 statutory overview, not a company tax calculation.
