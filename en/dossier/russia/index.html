I

In short

Conditional

Through 31 October 2026, Article 3a and the MFA page describe entry, transit and stays of up to 30 days for Russian citizens with a valid travel document issued by Russia’s competent authority, under bilateral agreements.[1], [2]

Trip dates and the document type and validity were not supplied; this is not an individual entry decision.
Not established

If a stay begins before 1 November 2026 and continues after that date, the sources found do not establish how the change applies to someone already admitted.[1]–[3]

Not established

The MFA announced a visa requirement from 1 November 2026. The complete procedure and exceptions for a particular trip require checking the operative Decree and agreement status.[1], [3], [4]
