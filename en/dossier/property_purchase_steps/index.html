I

In short

Conditional

Acquisition by transaction requires a registrable instrument; ownership arises on cadastral registration [1], [3]. Real estate transfer tax may apply to a purchase, with a separate exclusion for VAT-taxed transactions [4].[1]–[4]

Not established

Foreign buyer eligibility for a particular parcel requires its land classification and the current version of Article 415 [1], [2].[1]–[4]

IV

Not established

  • The parcel extract, encumbrances, planning and building status, seller authority and notarial form are unverified.
  • A foreign buyer needs an Article 415 check incorporating the 29/2025 amendment and parcel type; these sources do not establish blanket eligibility.
  • Financing, party identification, VAT treatment and local tax procedure have not been checked.
