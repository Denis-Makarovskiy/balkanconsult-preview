I

What is the answer?

Supported with limits

A Montenegro tax resident is within Article 4(1) for income earned both inside and outside Montenegro. A non-resident is within Article 4(2) for income earned in Montenegro [1]. Foreign tax may be creditable under Article 44, limited to the Montenegro tax on that income, and a tax treaty can take priority under Article 45 [1].[1], [2]

General resident/non-resident framework; no treaty or tax amount determined.

IV

What remains unknown?

  • The source country, income category, payer, period, tax residence, foreign tax paid and treaty are unknown.
  • A foreign bank transfer by itself does not establish source or taxability; the underlying income must be classified.
  • No treaty text, foreign tax evidence, social contributions or local surtax was checked; this is not a calculation of tax due.
