I

In short

Route-dependent answer

Montenegrin citizenship has different legal routes: descent and birth under limited conditions, admission on application, and specific rules for spouses or same-sex life partners. Article 8 provides the ordinary admission route; Article 11, as amended with effect from 19 August 2026, also covers a same-sex life partnership concluded under the special law. No single checklist decides every applicant's eligibility.[1]–[4]

Only the scope of checked sources is stated; no individual determination is made.
II

Rules

The checked 2020 MUP text sets out Articles 5–11. The adopted 2024 amendment changes Article 37 and the adopted 119/2026 amendment extends Article 11(1)–(2) to a same-sex life partnership concluded under the special law; the Gazette gives its effective date. These texts do not establish an individual's outcome.
