What visa rules apply to EU residents entering Montenegro?

I

What is the answer?

EU residence alone does not determine Montenegro’s visa rule. Nationality, travel document, and the issuing state, type and validity of the permit are needed.

Conditional

EU residence alone cannot answer whether a visa is required.[1]

Not established

The MFA describes a conditional route for a valid travel document plus a valid Schengen, US, UK or Irish visa or residence permit: up to 30 days, capped by the document’s expiry.[1]–[3]

II

What supports the answer?

The MFA overview [1] describes a document-based exception, not an entitlement for residence in every EU state. Article 7 of the 2019 Decree [2], text [3], is the base rule; the operative amended version has not been consolidated.

IV

What remains unknown?

  • PARTIAL/BLOCKED: nationality and the type and validity of the travel document are missing.
  • The issuing state, type and validity of any permit or visa are unknown; EU residence alone cannot be treated as a Schengen permit.
  • Travel dates and length are unknown; the operative Article 7 has not been consolidated.
