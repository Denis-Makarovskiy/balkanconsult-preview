I

In short

Conditional

Annual property tax is a local tax. The taxpayer is generally the owner on 1 January and the base is market value. The law sets a general 0.25–1% rate range, with special cases and reliefs [1].[1]

Not established

The actual amount depends on property characteristics and the current municipal decision [1].[1]
