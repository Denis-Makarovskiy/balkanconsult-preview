I

In short

Supported with limits

For an individual, Articles 37a–37h cover gains on a sale or transfer of real estate, a company interest or securities. The gain generally starts with the difference between sale and acquisition price, subject to the detailed valuation and exemption rules [1].[1], [2]

This is an individual capital-gains overview, not an assessment of a transaction.

IV

Not established

  • The asset, acquisition document, sale price, residence status and any Article 37g exception are unknown.
  • A company’s capital gains are governed by the corporate law, not this individual dossier. Cryptocurrency and other assets were not classified.
  • The January 2026 consolidation must be read with the later 70/26 amendment; that amendment concerns gambling income and Article 50f, not the cited capital-gain provisions.
